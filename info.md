# Enua Charge

Unofficial Home Assistant integration for Enua Charge EV chargers.

Signs in with your own Enua account (OAuth2 with PKCE — no client secret) and
creates one device per charger with sensors for vehicle state, power, session
energy, per-phase current and voltage, plus a charging switch and a max-current
control.

See the [README](https://github.com/stianandre/ha-enua) for setup requirements.
